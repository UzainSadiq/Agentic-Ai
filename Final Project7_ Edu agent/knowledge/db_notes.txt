Database Systems Notes

Normalization organizes relational data to reduce redundancy and update anomalies.
First Normal Form requires atomic values. Second Normal Form removes partial dependency
on a composite key. Third Normal Form removes transitive dependency.

A transaction should follow ACID properties: Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation and Durability.

Indexes can speed up reads but add storage and write-maintenance overhead. Primary keys
uniquely identify rows. Foreign keys enforce relationships between tables.
