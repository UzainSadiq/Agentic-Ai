Annual Technology Report 2025

ABC Technologies
January 2025

ABC Technologies is a software company headquartered in Lahore, Pakistan.
During 2025, the company expanded its artificial intelligence division and
introduced a new document automation platform.

Key information:
- The AI division grew by 28 percent.
- The company launched a document automation platform in January 2025.
- The engineering team expanded to 120 employees.
- The company plans to open a research office in Islamabad.

The report states that the main objective is to improve document processing,
information extraction, and workflow automation for business customers.
